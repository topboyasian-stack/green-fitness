# Green Fitness Performance Club

Mobile-first demo for Green Fitness, Ikotun, Lagos.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or paid service is required.

## Demo roles
- Member: digital membership card, simulated check-in, personal coaching profile, assigned programme, session completion and feedback.
- Trainer: member roster, coaching studio, individual programme builder, draft review/approval, trainer assignment and coaching notes.
- Owner: attendance, renewals and illustrative finance dashboard.
- Wellness: meal logging and simulated wearable telemetry.

## Personal coaching workflow
1. A member records their goal, experience, weekly availability, preferred session length, exercise preferences and limitations.
2. A trainer opens that member's profile and creates a distinct weekly programme.
3. The trainer can use a rule-based starter outline, then edit training days, exercises, sets, reps, rest and coaching cues.
4. Programmes can be saved as drafts or published to the member after trainer review.
5. Members see only their published plan in the member workspace, log completed sessions and send difficulty / discomfort feedback.
6. Trainers can review member feedback, including a pain/discomfort flag, and adjust the next programme.

Starter outlines are illustrative templates, not AI assessments or medical prescriptions. Trainers remain responsible for reviewing exercise suitability, technique, training loads and progression. Members should stop and contact their trainer if they experience pain or unusual symptoms.

## Demo limitations
All people, financial figures and telemetry in this demo are fictional. Member switching is a preview control, not authentication. QR check-in and wearable data are simulations, not live integrations. Coaching records, notes, programmes and feedback use browser local storage; they do not sync between devices or provide secure multi-user access.

This prototype is not ready for real member records or payments. Production requires secure authentication, server-side role permissions, a production database, consent and privacy controls, audit logs, backups and verified payment webhooks.
